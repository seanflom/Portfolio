About Me

I am a senior-year student majoring in Geography (B.A) with minors in Geospatial Analysis and Environmental Planning at Cal Poly Humboldt. I am passionate about all things related the representation and analysis of spatial data, particularly as they relate to the built environment. In addition to GIScience, I have studied biology, botany, chemistry, and forestry.
